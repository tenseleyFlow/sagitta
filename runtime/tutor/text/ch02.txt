YEW TUTOR 2/7 — UNITS, SELECTIONS, AND CURSORS

L, W, and B are layers. The arrows keep their direction while the unit they
cross changes. H borrows the unit you came from and extends a selection.

[ ] ch02.t1 Press w to enter W mode.
[ ] ch02.t2 Move Right once to the next word.
[ ] ch02.t3 Delete the word under the cursor with d.
[ ] ch02.t4 Press b, then Down to visit the next block.
[ ] ch02.t5 Press h and extend a line selection with Shift-Down.
[ ] ch02.t6 Press Enter to lift the selected lines to cursors.
[ ] ch02.t7 Type ! once and see every cursor edit in one transaction.
