YEW TUTOR 6/7 — RECORDINGS ARE FLETCH

A recording stores commands, not key bytes. Rebinding a key therefore does
not change what an old recording means.

[ ] ch06.t1 Start recording register a with q a.
[ ] ch06.t2 Insert X at the start of the title.
[ ] ch06.t3 Stop recording with q.
[ ] ch06.t4 Return to the title start and replay with g g then @ a.
[ ] ch06.t5 Open the macro browser with Q; open a's source with e.
[ ] ch06.t6 Change the recorded X to Y and store it with s.
[ ] ch06.t7 Return to the tutor group with Ctrl-1; go home and replay @a.
